I'm a graduate student in the [Honors M.A. Statistics](https://stat.columbia.edu/) program at **Columbia University** (expected December 2026), working at the intersection of machine learning, statistics, and natural language processing.

Before Columbia, I completed dual B.S. degrees in Mathematics-Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at **Brown University** (GPA 3.7, 2024).

## Research

My work focuses on transformer interpretability, multimodal models, and Bayesian statistical methods. Past projects include:

- **UCSF** — Bayesian models for familial frontotemporal dementia progression (R, Stan, JAGS)
- **Brown LUNAR Lab** (Ellie Pavlick) — Representational alignment in multimodal vision-language models (honors thesis)
- **Cornell** (Carla Gomes) — Attention-based GNNs for combinatorial optimization
- **Brown** (Jon Roth) — Econometrics research on log-linear regression limitations
